## /proxy works from a player on another address, and gives up on a stalled upstream

**`/proxy`** — the route for addon streams that need their own request headers. A player opened on
a home-network address, or on another port of the server's name, now counts as the server's own
page, so those streams keep reaching addresses on your network; before, they got a 403. An upstream
now has 30 seconds to connect and answer, redirects included, and to deliver a playlist; after that
the answer is 504, so a stalled upstream no longer holds a place. `r` may no longer set `Location`,
and two cloud-metadata addresses outside link-local are refused like link-local.

**Upgrade:** pull it.
