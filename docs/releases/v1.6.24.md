## Keep measures a title before keeping it

A kept (pinned) title is never evicted, so keeping one is refused when it would leave too little disk
for normal streaming. That check could not see a magnet's size before its details arrived and
counted it as nothing, so such a title was kept unmeasured; and for a title nobody had narrowed it
counted only what playback had fetched so far, though keeping it fetches every file. Keep now waits
briefly for a magnet's details and otherwise answers "try again" (`409 size_unknown`), measures the
whole title unless it was narrowed to one file, and a refusal states the full space it needs.

**Upgrade:** pull it.
