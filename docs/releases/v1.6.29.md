## A title you are watching is not "downloaded"

On the library page, a title the player was still fetching — a film halfway through, say — sat on
the Downloaded shelf as "complete". It now shows on the Downloading shelf with its progress and
speed ("playing", or "fetching" once you stop and it carries on in the background), and a title left
partly fetched says how much of it is there (`partial 45%`).

**Upgrade:** pull it.
