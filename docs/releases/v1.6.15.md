## Styled subtitles on the TV

A Stremio TV app built on stremio-video 0.0.97 or later can now draw a file's own ASS/SSA subtitles
with their styles and fonts. With the player's ASS subtitles styling setting on, each such track
shows as a second entry, marked "(styled)", next to the TV's plain one. The server reads only the
stretch being watched, a minute at a time, and the TV's own playback keeps priority over it.

Older TV apps, and every browser and desktop player, are unchanged.

**Upgrade:** pull it.
