## Gets its TV certificate back by itself

With `IPADDRESS` set, a server that could not get the trusted `*.stremio.rocks` certificate at
startup now asks again every 30 minutes and switches to it the moment it arrives — no restart from
you. Meanwhile it says why, in one plain log line and in `/health` (`certStatus`), and reports
itself degraded, since TVs cannot connect until then.

**Upgrade:** pull it.
